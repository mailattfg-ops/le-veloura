The Estate

More than a venue —
a setting for the moments
you'll remember.

Le Veloura was envisioned as a place where every celebration is held with equal reverence — where the grandeur of a wedding morning and the warmth of a family gathering both feel entirely at home. Set across a generous 1.5-acre estate near Calicut, the property offers four distinct spaces, each designed with intention.

Positioned in Thadampattuthazham, Kozhikode, and a comfortable 40-minute drive from Calicut International Airport, Le Veloura blends the ease of accessibility with the seclusion of a private retreat.

Aurelius

The Signature Celebration Space

Spacious, elegant, and designed to host celebrations at the grandest scale, the Aurelius is the heart of the convention centre — a premium venue crafted for unforgettable weddings, corporate gatherings, and luxury celebrations.

SettingIndoor Event Hall
CapacityCapacity: 800 – 1000 Guests

The Promenade

Outdoor Lawn

Surrounded by lush greenery, warm architectural lighting, and elegant design, The Promenade is an intimate outdoor celebration space. It creates a serene atmosphere perfect for engagement ceremonies, reception dinners, and private gatherings under the open sky.

SettingOutdoor Garden
CapacityCapacity: 200 Guests

The Lumiere

The Glass Pavilion

Elegant, intimate, and beautifully illuminated, The Lumiere is a refined celebration space. Surrounded by floor-to-ceiling glass architecture and warm chandelier lighting, the venue creates a luxurious ambience that feels both modern and timeless.

SettingIndoor Lounge
CapacityCapacity: 150 Guests

Celestia

Dining Hall

Designed to bring people together through memorable dining experiences, Celestia is an elegant dining hall where contemporary architecture meets warmth and comfort. Surrounded by expansive glass panels, it offers a seamless blend of indoor sophistication and open-air charm.

SettingIndoor Dining
CapacityCapacity: 300 Guests

Culinary Infrastructure

Our full-fledged commercial kitchen is at the service of caterers of your choice. We also offer priority deals with trusted catering partners to ensure every plate carries the same intention as every other detail of your event.
